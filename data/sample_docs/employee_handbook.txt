ACME Corp Employee Handbook (Sample)

Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break. Remote work is permitted
up to three days per week with manager approval.

Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Annual leave must be requested at least two weeks in advance.
Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Sick leave is separate and capped at 12 days per year.

Refund and Reimbursement Policy
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred.
Reimbursement requests require an itemized receipt. Travel expenses
booked through the company portal are reimbursed automatically.
Refunds for cancelled business travel are processed within 14 business days.

Equipment Policy
Each employee is issued a laptop and a monitor. Equipment must be returned
when leaving the company. Damaged equipment should be reported to IT within
48 hours. Personal use of company equipment should be kept to a minimum.

Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect. Harassment of
any kind is not tolerated. Confidential company information must not be
shared outside the organization.
